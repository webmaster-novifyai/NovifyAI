Returns & Exchanges

Our Return Luxury Window

We provide a 14-day tracking window from the exact delivery timestamp to process return or adjustment exchange requests. All merchandise must remain entirely unworn, unaltered, and with original structural brand tags completely intact.

Exclusions

Custom tailored couture items, modified luxury evening dresses, or specific limited items purchased under promotional sales parameters are not eligible for standard return processing.

Processing Refunds

Once our validation depot receives and inspects the condition of your package, your credit ledger balance will be restored automatically back to your payment account within 5-7 business processing days.
